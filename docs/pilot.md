# Support team interview

Ask about the team's most recent support policy change before showing the demo.

1. What changed, when did it take effect, and which existing customers kept the old terms?
2. Walk through the checks someone actually ran. How did they verify the order or account action?
3. Which cases took the most time? Did anyone discover a missed case after release?
4. Show the two-shopper demo. Which evidence would you need to investigate or dismiss the reported denial?
5. Could the team replay a past policy edit using synthetic orders in staging? Agree on who would independently verify each outcome.

Record observed facts, open questions, and any agreed next step separately.
